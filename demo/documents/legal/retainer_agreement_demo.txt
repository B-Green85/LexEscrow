THIS RETAINER AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 12, 2026,

90002 (the "Client").

1. ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION

1.1 The Client hereby retains the Attorney to provide legal representation in
connection with a commercial contract dispute arising from that certain Master
Services Agreement dated June 4, 2024, between the Client and Pinnacle Logistics
Systems LLC (the "Matter").

1.2 The scope of the engagement includes: (a) pre-litigation demand and
negotiation; (b) preparation and filing of a complaint in the Superior Court of
Clearwater County, if warranted; (c) written discovery and document production;
(d) motion practice through the dispositive motion stage; and (e) participation
in court-ordered mediation.

1.3 The engagement expressly EXCLUDES trial representation, appellate work,
bankruptcy proceedings, and any matter arising outside the Matter described in
Section 1.1. Any expansion of scope requires a written amendment signed by both
parties.

2. RETAINER DEPOSIT

2.1 Upon execution of this Agreement, the Client shall deposit with the Attorney
the sum of EIGHTEEN THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED AND 00/100 DOLLARS as an advance
against fees and costs.

    Total Retainer: $18,500.00

2.2 As of the date of the most recent statement rendered under Section 4, the
following amounts apply to the retainer:

    Earned Fees to Date: $6,200.00
    Unearned Balance Held in Trust: $12,300.00

2.3 The retainer is not a flat fee, is not a bonus, and is not earned upon
receipt. The retainer remains the property of the Client until earned by the
Attorney through the performance of legal services.

3. FEES AND BILLING TERMS

3.1 Legal services shall be billed at the following hourly rates:

    Robert K. Langley, Partner ................ $425.00 per hour
    Associate Attorney ........................ $265.00 per hour
    Paralegal / Law Clerk ..................... $145.00 per hour

3.2 Time is recorded in increments of one-tenth (0.1) of an hour. The minimum
billing increment for any single task is 0.1 hour.

3.3 The Client shall additionally be responsible for costs advanced, including
filing fees, service of process, court reporter fees, expert fees, and
electronic research charges. Costs are billed at the Attorney's actual out-of-
pocket expense without markup.

3.4 Statements shall be rendered monthly. Each statement shall itemize services
by date, timekeeper, narrative description, hours expended, applicable rate, and
resulting charge, together with the beginning and ending trust balance for the
period.

4. TRUST ACCOUNT AND IOLTA PROVISIONS

4.1 All advanced fees and costs shall be deposited into the Attorney's client
trust account maintained at Harborview Community Bank, Account No. ending 4417,
an Interest on Lawyers' Trust Accounts ("IOLTA") account established in
accordance with the rules of the State Bar of California.

4.2 Funds shall be withdrawn from trust only after services have been rendered
and a statement reflecting such services has been transmitted to the Client. No
funds shall be withdrawn in advance of services performed.

4.3 The Attorney shall maintain complete records of all trust account
transactions for the Matter and shall render a written accounting to the Client
upon request, upon termination of the representation, and at the conclusion of
the Matter.

4.4 Interest accruing on IOLTA deposits is remitted to the State Bar foundation
in accordance with applicable rules and does not accrue to the benefit of either
the Client or the Attorney.

5. TERMINATION

5.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause,
by written notice delivered to the Attorney at the address stated above.

5.2 The Attorney may withdraw from the representation to the extent permitted by
the applicable rules of professional conduct, including upon the Client's failure
to replenish the retainer or to cooperate in the Matter.

5.3 Upon termination from any cause, the Attorney shall, within fourteen (14)
calendar days: (a) render a final accounting; (b) refund to the Client any
unearned portion of the retainer then remaining in trust; and (c) deliver the
complete client file to the Client or to successor counsel as directed.

5.4 Termination does not relieve the Client of the obligation to pay for services
actually rendered and costs actually advanced prior to the effective date of
termination.

6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
California without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

6.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding of the
parties and supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only in a writing
signed by both parties.

6.3 Arbitration of Fee Disputes. Any dispute concerning fees shall first be
submitted to non-binding fee arbitration under the applicable State Bar program
before any other proceeding is commenced.

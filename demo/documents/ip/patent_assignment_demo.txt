THIS PATENT ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "Assignment") is made and entered into as
of April 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between QUARTZ INNOVATIONS LLC, a
limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, with
offices at 3300 Ironwood Commons, Suite 140, Demo City, Delaware 19804
("Assignor"), and MERIDIAN TECH HOLDINGS, a corporation organized under the laws
of the State of Delaware, with offices at 1725 Palisade Center Drive, Demo City,
Delaware 19805 ("Assignee").

RECITALS

A. Assignor is the sole owner of record of the Assigned Patent identified in
Section 1, together with all rights, title, and interest therein.

B. Assignee desires to acquire all right, title, and interest in and to the
Assigned Patent, and Assignor desires to assign the same, upon the terms and
conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and
other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are
hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. ASSIGNED PATENT

1.1 The patent assigned hereunder (the "Assigned Patent") is:

    Patent Number: US 10,999,999 B2
    Title: Adaptive Thermal Regulation System for Modular Enclosures
    Issue Date: January 14, 2025
    Application Serial No.: 17/884,206
    Filing Date: March 3, 2022
    Named Inventors: Sylvane O. Kettridge and Amara J. Blackwood-Reyes
    Current Assignee of Record: Quartz Innovations LLC

1.2 The Assigned Patent covers a closed-loop thermal management system employing
distributed sensing and predictive control for modular electronic enclosures,
including the apparatus, method, and control system claims set forth in claims 1
through 24 thereof.

2. ASSIGNMENT

2.1 Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, and conveys to
Assignee, its successors and assigns, the entire right, title, and interest
throughout the world in and to the Assigned Patent, including:

    (a) the Assigned Patent and all inventions and improvements disclosed
        therein;

    (b) all reissues, reexaminations, extensions, continuations,
        continuations-in-part, divisionals, and substitutions thereof;

    (c) all foreign counterparts and all rights of priority under the
        international conventions;

    (d) all causes of action, whether accrued before or after the Effective Date,
        for past, present, and future infringement, together with the right to
        sue for and collect all damages, profits, and other remedies; and

    (e) all rights to license, sublicense, enforce, and otherwise exploit the
        foregoing.

2.2 Assignor authorizes and requests the Commissioner for Patents to record this
Assignment and to issue any and all patents arising from the foregoing to
Assignee as assignee of the entire right, title, and interest.

4. ONGOING ROYALTY

4.1 In addition to the Assignment Price, Assignee shall pay to Assignor a
continuing royalty equal to four percent (4%) of Net Revenue actually received by
Assignee from the commercialization, licensing, or sublicensing of the Assigned
Patent during the Royalty Term.

4.2 "Net Revenue" means gross amounts invoiced and actually collected by Assignee
attributable to products or licenses covered by one or more valid claims of the
Assigned Patent, less returns, allowances, sales and use taxes, and freight and
insurance separately stated on the invoice.

4.3 The "Royalty Term" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and
ending upon expiration of the last-to-expire valid claim of the Assigned Patent.

4.4 Royalty payments shall be made quarterly, within forty-five (45) days
following the close of each calendar quarter, accompanied by a written statement
setting forth Net Revenue for the quarter and the calculation of the amount due.

4.5 MINIMUM ANNUAL ROYALTY. Notwithstanding actual Net Revenue, Assignee shall
pay Assignor a minimum royalty of $12,000.00 per calendar year commencing with
calendar year 2027, creditable against royalties otherwise payable for that year.

4.6 Where Assignee sublicenses the Assigned Patent to a third party, the royalty
payable to Assignor on amounts received from such sublicense shall be calculated
at a rate of 4.5% of the consideration received by Assignee under the sublicense,
payable on the same quarterly schedule set forth in Section 4.4.

4.7 AUDIT. Assignor may, not more than once per calendar year and upon thirty
(30) days' prior written notice, cause an independent certified public accountant
to examine Assignee's records relating to royalties for the preceding two (2)
calendar years. The audit shall be at Assignor's expense unless it discloses an
underpayment exceeding five percent (5%) for the period examined, in which case
Assignee shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit and shall promptly remit the
underpaid amount together with interest at nine percent (9%) per annum.

5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

5.1 Assignor represents and warrants that: (a) it is the sole and exclusive owner
of the entire right, title, and interest in the Assigned Patent; (b) the Assigned
Patent is free and clear of all liens, security interests, licenses, and
encumbrances; (c) Assignor has full right and authority to enter into this
Assignment; (d) all maintenance fees due as of the Effective Date have been
timely paid; (e) no action, proceeding, opposition, interference, reexamination,
or post-grant review is pending or, to Assignor's knowledge, threatened with
respect to the Assigned Patent; and (f) Assignor has not received any written
notice alleging that the Assigned Patent is invalid or unenforceable.

5.2 Assignor has disclosed to Assignee all prior art known to Assignor that is
material to the patentability of the claims of the Assigned Patent.

6. FURTHER ASSURANCES

6.1 Assignor shall, at Assignee's request and expense, execute all further
documents and take all further actions reasonably necessary to perfect, record,
maintain, defend, or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned Patent, including

6.2 Assignor shall cooperate in good faith with Assignee in the prosecution of
any pending or future application claiming priority to the Assigned Patent and in
any enforcement action, and shall make the named inventors reasonably available
for such purposes.

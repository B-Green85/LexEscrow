PATENT LICENSE AGREEMENT

DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL PARTIES - NOT A LEGAL INSTRUMENT

Agreement No.: PLA-2026-10999999-BMC
Effective Date: June 15, 2026

THIS PATENT LICENSE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of June 15,
2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERIDIAN TECH HOLDINGS, a Delaware
corporation with offices at 1725 Palisade Center Drive, Demo City, Delaware 19805
("Licensor"), and BRIGHTPATH MANUFACTURING CO., a corporation organized under the
laws of the State of Delaware, with offices at 6100 Ridgemont Industrial Parkway,
Demo City, Delaware 19807 ("Licensee").

RECITALS

A. Licensor is the owner of record of the Licensed Patent, having acquired the
same by assignment recorded with the United States Patent and Trademark Office.

B. Licensee desires to obtain, and Licensor is willing to grant, a license under
the Licensed Patent within the Field of Use and Territory defined herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Licensed Patent" means:

    Patent Number: US 10,999,999 B2
    Title: Adaptive Thermal Regulation System for Modular Enclosures
    Issue Date: January 14, 2025
    Application Serial No.: 17/884,206
    Named Inventors: Sylvane O. Kettridge and Amara J. Blackwood-Reyes

together with all reissues, reexaminations, extensions, continuations,
continuations-in-part, and divisionals thereof.

1.2 "Licensed Product" means any product, system, subassembly, or component
manufactured, used, sold, offered for sale, or imported by Licensee that is
covered by one or more Valid Claims of the Licensed Patent.

1.3 "Valid Claim" means a claim of the Licensed Patent that has not been held
invalid or unenforceable by a decision of a tribunal of competent jurisdiction
from which no appeal has been or can be taken, and that has not been disclaimed,
cancelled, or abandoned.

1.4 "Net Sales" means gross amounts invoiced by Licensee for Licensed Products
sold to unaffiliated third parties, less: (a) documented trade and quantity
discounts actually allowed; (b) credits for returns and rejected goods; (c)
sales, use, and value-added taxes separately stated on the invoice; and (d)
outbound freight and insurance separately stated on the invoice.

1.5 "Contract Year" means each successive twelve (12) month period commencing on
the Effective Date or on any anniversary thereof.

2. GRANT OF LICENSE

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement and to Licensee's timely payment of
all amounts due, Licensor hereby grants to Licensee a non-exclusive,
non-transferable license under the Licensed Patent to make, have made, use,
offer for sale, sell, and import Licensed Products solely within the Field of Use
and the Territory.

2.2 SUBLICENSING. Licensee shall have no right to grant sublicenses without the
prior written consent of Licensor, which consent may be withheld in Licensor's
sole discretion. Any purported sublicense granted without such consent is void.

2.3 HAVE-MADE RIGHTS. Licensee may engage contract manufacturers to produce
Licensed Products exclusively for Licensee's account, provided that Licensee
remains fully responsible for such manufacturers' compliance with this Agreement
and provides Licensor with written notice identifying each such manufacturer.

2.4 RESERVATION. All rights not expressly granted herein are reserved to
Licensor. No license is granted by implication, estoppel, or otherwise under any
patent, know-how, trademark, or other intellectual property of Licensor other
than the Licensed Patent.

3. FIELD OF USE

3.1 The license granted in Section 2.1 is limited to the design, manufacture, and
sale of thermal regulation subsystems incorporated into industrial modular
enclosures rated for continuous operation between minus twenty (-20) degrees
Celsius and sixty-five (65) degrees Celsius, for deployment in stationary
telecommunications, data infrastructure, and industrial automation cabinets (the
"Field of Use").

3.2 The Field of Use expressly EXCLUDES: (a) automotive, aerospace, marine, and
other mobile platform applications; (b) medical devices of any classification;
(c) consumer electronics; and (d) any application involving human life support.

4. TERRITORY

4.1 The license granted herein extends to the following territory (the
"Territory"): the Republic of Astoria, the Federated States of Marisol, the
Kingdom of Verdanne, and the Northern Kestrel Territories.

4.2 Licensee shall not manufacture, sell, offer for sale, or knowingly permit the
resale of Licensed Products outside the Territory, and shall include contractual
territorial restrictions in its distributor agreements.

5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless earlier terminated
under Section 9, continues until the expiration of the last-to-expire Valid Claim
of the Licensed Patent (the "Term").

5.2 The initial committed period is five (5) Contract Years. Thereafter the
Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either
party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days before
the end of the then-current period.

6. ROYALTIES AND PAYMENT

6.1 RUNNING ROYALTY. Licensee shall pay Licensor a running royalty of five and
one-half percent (5.5%) of Net Sales of Licensed Products during the Term.

6.2 LICENSE ISSUE FEE. Licensee shall pay Licensor a one-time, non-refundable
license issue fee of $75,000.00 within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.
The license issue fee is creditable against running royalties payable in the
first Contract Year only.

6.3 MINIMUM ANNUAL ROYALTY. Licensee shall pay a minimum annual royalty of
$40,000.00 for each Contract Year, commencing with the second Contract Year. The
minimum annual royalty is creditable against running royalties otherwise accruing
in the same Contract Year. Any shortfall shall be paid within forty-five (45)
days following the close of the Contract Year.

    License Issue Fee: $75,000.00
    Running Royalty Rate: 5.5% of Net Sales
    Minimum Annual Royalty: $40,000.00 per Contract Year

6.4 PAYMENT TERMS. Running royalties accrue upon invoicing of Licensed Products
and are payable quarterly, within forty-five (45) days following the close of
each calendar quarter, in United States Dollars by wire transfer to an account
designated by Licensor.

6.5 LATE PAYMENT. Amounts not paid when due bear interest at one and one-half
percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less,
from the due date until paid.

6.6 TAXES. All amounts are exclusive of taxes. Licensee is responsible for any
withholding tax imposed on payments hereunder and shall gross up payments so that
Licensor receives the amount it would have received absent such withholding.

7. REPORTING AND AUDIT RIGHTS

7.1 QUARTERLY REPORTS. Within forty-five (45) days following the close of each
calendar quarter, Licensee shall deliver a written royalty report stating, for
the quarter: (a) units of each Licensed Product sold, by product designation and
by country within the Territory; (b) gross invoiced amounts; (c) each deduction
claimed under Section 1.4, itemized by category; (d) resulting Net Sales; (e) the
royalty calculation; and (f) cumulative amounts for the Contract Year to date.

7.2 A report is due for each quarter whether or not any royalty is payable.

7.3 RECORDS. Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records
sufficient to verify royalty calculations for a period of five (5) years
following the end of the Contract Year to which they relate.

7.4 AUDIT. Licensor may, not more than once per Contract Year and upon thirty
(30) days' prior written notice, cause an independent certified public accountant
reasonably acceptable to Licensee to inspect Licensee's records during normal
business hours for the purpose of verifying royalty reports for the preceding
three (3) Contract Years. The auditor shall report to Licensor only the amount of
any discrepancy.

7.5 AUDIT COSTS. Licensor bears the cost of the audit unless the audit discloses
an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%) of amounts due for the period
examined, in which case Licensee shall reimburse the reasonable cost of the audit
and pay the underpaid amount with interest under Section 6.5 within thirty (30)
days.

8. MARKING, QUALITY, AND ENFORCEMENT

8.1 Licensee shall mark all Licensed Products with the notice "U.S. Pat.
10,999,999" or with a virtual marking reference in accordance with applicable
law.

8.2 Licensed Products shall conform to commercially reasonable quality standards
and to all applicable safety and regulatory requirements in the Territory.

8.3 Each party shall promptly notify the other in writing of any known or
suspected infringement of the Licensed Patent within the Field of Use. Licensor
retains the sole right, but not the obligation, to institute and control any
enforcement action. Licensee shall cooperate at Licensor's expense.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 TERMINATION FOR BREACH. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon
written notice if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure the
breach within sixty (60) days after written notice describing it, provided that
the cure period for non-payment is thirty (30) days.

9.2 TERMINATION FOR INSOLVENCY. Either party may terminate immediately upon
written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment
for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed over a substantial part
of its assets.

9.3 TERMINATION FOR CHALLENGE. Licensor may terminate immediately upon written
notice if Licensee, directly or through an affiliate, institutes or supports any
proceeding challenging the validity or enforceability of the Licensed Patent.

9.4 TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Licensee may terminate at the close of any
Contract Year upon not less than one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written
notice, provided that all minimum annual royalties accrued through the effective
date of termination are paid in full.

9.5 EFFECT OF TERMINATION. Upon termination: (a) all licenses granted terminate
immediately; (b) all accrued but unpaid amounts become due within thirty (30)
days; (c) Licensee shall deliver a final royalty report within forty-five (45)
days; and (d) Licensee may, during a sell-off period of one hundred twenty (120)
days, dispose of finished Licensed Products in inventory as of the termination
date, subject to payment of royalties thereon.

9.6 SURVIVAL. Sections 1, 6.4 through 6.6, 7.3 through 7.5, 9.5, 9.6, and 10
survive termination.

10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

10.2 Assignment. Licensee may not assign this Agreement, by operation of law or
otherwise, without Licensor's prior written consent. Licensor may assign freely
to a successor to the Licensed Patent.

10.3 Notices. Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses stated
above, effective upon confirmed receipt.

10.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the
parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings.

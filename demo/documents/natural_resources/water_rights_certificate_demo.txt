2. PRIORITY

2.1 Priority Date: April 18, 1912

2.2 The right evidenced by this certificate is a senior appropriative right under
the doctrine of prior appropriation as administered in the State of Arvada, and
ranks ahead of all rights bearing a later priority date within the Bittercreek
subbasin.

2.3 Adjudication Reference: Bittercreek Basin General Adjudication, Decree No.
CV-1974-0088, Redstone County District Court, entered September 9, 1974.

3. SOURCE AND POINT OF DIVERSION

3.1 Source of Supply: Bittercreek River, a tributary of the Redstone River,
within the Bittercreek subbasin.

3.2 Point of Diversion: The Marchbanks Headgate, located in the NE 1/4 of the
SW 1/4 of Section 22, Township 6 North, Range 14 West, Redstone County, State of
Arvada, at a point approximately 1,320 feet north and 890 feet east of the
southwest corner of said Section 22.

3.3 Diversion Works: One concrete headgate structure with a rated capacity of
28.0 cubic feet per second, together with the Bittercreek Lateral No. 3 conveyance
ditch and appurtenant measuring device (Parshall flume, 4-foot throat).

3.4 Alternate Point of Diversion: The Sagebrush Pump Station, located in the SE
1/4 of the NW 1/4 of Section 27, Township 6 North, Range 14 West, authorized for
use only when flows at the primary point fall below 6.0 cubic feet per second.

4. QUANTITY AND ALLOCATION

    Annual Allocation: 7,200 acre-feet per calendar year
    Maximum Diversion Rate: 24.0 cubic feet per second
    Irrigated Acreage Served: 2,400 acres
    Duty of Water: 3.0 acre-feet per acre per year
    Historical Consumptive Use: 4,320 acre-feet per year

5. PERMITTED USE AND PLACE OF USE

5.1 Permitted Use: Irrigation of agricultural lands, together with incidental
stock watering not exceeding 40 acre-feet per year.

5.2 Season of Use: March 15 through October 31 of each calendar year.

5.3 Place of Use: 2,400 acres situated within Sections 21, 22, 27, and 28,
Township 6 North, Range 14 West, Redstone County, State of Arvada, as more
particularly described in the map on file with the Department under this
certificate number.

5.4 Return Flow Obligation: Not less than 30 percent of diverted water shall be
returned to the Bittercreek River at or above the confluence with Sagebrush
Draw.

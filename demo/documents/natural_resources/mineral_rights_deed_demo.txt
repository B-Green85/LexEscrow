1. CONVEYANCE

FOR AND IN CONSIDERATION of the sum of TEN AND 00/100 DOLLARS ($10.00) and other
good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby
acknowledged, CLEARWATER AGRICULTURAL LLC, an Arvada limited liability company,
whose address is 1440 Bittercreek Road, Redstone County, State of Arvada 87410
("Grantor"), does hereby GRANT, BARGAIN, SELL, AND CONVEY unto HIGHPLAINS FARMING
CO., an Arvada corporation, whose address is 705 Dryland Junction Road, Redstone
County, State of Arvada 87412 ("Grantee"), subject to the reservations and
limitations set forth herein, all of Grantor's right, title, and interest in and
to the oil, gas, and other minerals in, on, and under the lands described in
Section 3 below.

2. SEVERANCE OF THE MINERAL ESTATE

2.1 By this instrument the mineral estate is hereby SEVERED from the surface
estate in the lands described in Section 3, and shall hereafter constitute a
separate and distinct estate in real property, separately assessed and separately
conveyable.

2.2 The mineral estate conveyed includes all oil, gas, casinghead gas,
condensate, coal, lignite, sulphur, potash, sodium, uranium, geothermal
resources, and all other minerals of every kind and character, whether now known
or hereafter discovered, subject to the depth limitation in Section 5.

2.3 The mineral estate conveyed includes the exclusive right to explore for,
develop, drill for, produce, extract, store, transport, and market the minerals
so conveyed, together with the right to grant oil and gas leases and to receive
all bonuses, delay rentals, and royalties thereunder, subject to Grantor's
reserved royalty under Section 4.

2.4 The surface estate in the lands described in Section 3 is retained by Grantor
and is not conveyed by this instrument.

3. LEGAL DESCRIPTION OF THE LANDS

described as follows:

4. RESERVATION OF ROYALTY

4.1 Grantor hereby EXCEPTS AND RESERVES unto itself, its successors and assigns,
a perpetual nonparticipating royalty interest equal to three percent (3%) of the
gross proceeds received from the sale of all oil, gas, and other minerals
produced, saved, and marketed from the lands described in Section 3 (the
"Reserved Royalty").

4.2 The Reserved Royalty is a nonparticipating interest. Grantor shall have no
right to execute oil and gas leases, no right to receive bonus or delay rental
payments, and no right to participate in the management or development of the
mineral estate.

4.3 The Reserved Royalty shall be free and clear of all costs of exploration,
drilling, development, and production, but shall bear its proportionate share of
severance, production, and ad valorem taxes attributable thereto.

4.4 Payments on account of the Reserved Royalty shall be made monthly, on or
before the last day of the second month following the month of production,
accompanied by a statement of volumes produced, prices received, and deductions
taken.

5. DEPTH LIMITATIONS

5.1 The mineral estate conveyed by this instrument is LIMITED TO those depths
lying below a subsurface depth of five hundred (500) feet beneath the surface of
the lands described in Section 3, as measured from the ground surface at each
point.

5.2 Grantor EXCEPTS AND RESERVES all minerals, including sand, gravel, caliche,
building stone, and other surface construction materials, lying above a
subsurface depth of five hundred (500) feet, together with the exclusive right to
extract the same for use on the lands or for sale.

5.3 The mineral estate conveyed is FURTHER LIMITED at the base to a subsurface
depth of twelve thousand (12,000) feet. All minerals lying below 12,000 feet are
excepted and reserved unto Grantor.

6. SURFACE USE AGREEMENT

6.1 Grantee's right of access to and use of the surface estate for mineral
development is subject to the following limitations, which run with the land and
bind Grantee's successors, assigns, and lessees:

6.2 NOTICE. Grantee shall give Grantor not less than sixty (60) days' written
notice prior to commencing any surface-disturbing operation, specifying the
proposed location, the area to be disturbed, and the anticipated duration.

6.3 LOCATION RESTRICTIONS. No well pad, road, tank battery, compressor, or
gathering facility shall be located: (a) within one thousand (1,000) feet of any
existing residence, barn, or livestock facility; (b) within five hundred (500)
feet of the Bittercreek River or of any irrigation ditch, lateral, or headgate;
(c) within three hundred (300) feet of any water well; or (d) within any area
under center-pivot irrigation, except upon Grantor's prior written consent.

6.4 SURFACE DAMAGES. Grantee shall pay Grantor surface damages at the rate of
$4,200.00 per acre for each acre taken out of agricultural production for the
duration of the disturbance, together with a one-time payment of $1,800.00 per
well site for construction impacts, and shall compensate Grantor for actual loss
of growing crops at then-current market value.

6.5 ROADS AND FENCES. Grantee shall use existing ranch roads where practicable,
shall maintain all roads used in a condition equal to or better than that
existing prior to use, shall install and maintain cattle guards or gates at every
fence crossing, and shall keep all gates closed.

6.6 WATER. Grantee shall not use water from Grantor's water right, Certificate
No. ARV-WR-88-14207, or from any well, spring, or impoundment on the lands, for
drilling, completion, or operations, without Grantor's separate written
agreement.

6.7 RECLAMATION. Upon cessation of operations at any site, Grantee shall remove
all equipment and debris, plug and abandon wells in accordance with applicable
regulation, restore the surface contour, replace segregated topsoil, and reseed
with a seed mixture approved by Grantor. Reclamation shall be completed within
twelve (12) months of cessation.

6.8 BOND. Grantee shall maintain a surface reclamation bond in favor of Grantor
in the amount of $150,000.00 for so long as any surface disturbance remains
unreclaimed.

7. WARRANTY

7.1 Grantor warrants and shall forever defend title to the mineral estate hereby
conveyed unto Grantee against every person lawfully claiming or to claim the same
or any part thereof, by, through, or under Grantor, but not otherwise.

7.2 Grantor represents that the mineral estate is free of all liens and
encumbrances created by Grantor, and that no oil and gas lease affecting the
lands is presently in force.

8. HABENDUM

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the mineral estate hereby conveyed, together with all and
singular the rights and appurtenances thereto belonging, unto Grantee, its
successors and assigns forever, subject to the reservations, exceptions, and
limitations set forth herein.

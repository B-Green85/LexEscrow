2. STATEMENT OF AUTHENTICITY

2.1 The Halloway-Brenner Fine Art Authentication Bureau hereby certifies that the
work described in Section 1 is an authentic and original painting executed by the
hand of the artist M. Delacroix in the year 1974.

2.2 This certification is issued following physical examination of the work,
comparative stylistic analysis, technical imaging, and review of documentary
provenance as set forth in Sections 3 and 4.

4. BASIS OF AUTHENTICATION

4.1 PHYSICAL EXAMINATION. The work was examined under natural light, raking
light, ultraviolet fluorescence, and infrared reflectography on January 22, 2026
at the Bureau's laboratory.

4.2 SUPPORT AND GROUND. The canvas is a medium-weight linen, hand-stretched over
a five-member keyed pine stretcher consistent with the artist's documented studio
practice between 1969 and 1979. Tack margins are original and unlined.

4.3 PIGMENT ANALYSIS. Non-destructive X-ray fluorescence spectroscopy at eleven
sample points identified a pigment profile consistent with the artist's
documented palette of the period, including the cobalt and ultramarine
formulations characteristic of the Blue Compositions series. No anachronistic
pigment was detected.

4.4 UNDERDRAWING. Infrared reflectography disclosed a rapid charcoal underdrawing
with two pentimenti in the upper left quadrant, consistent with the working
method documented in the artist's surviving studio photographs.

4.5 SIGNATURE ANALYSIS. The signature was compared against fourteen exemplar
signatures from documented works of the period 1971 to 1977. Letterform,
pressure, and stroke sequence are consistent with the exemplars.

4.6 LITERATURE. The work is recorded in the catalogue raisonne of the artist,
Volume II, Catalogue Number 214, with a black-and-white reproduction at plate 87.

4.7 The Bureau found no evidence of restoration beyond minor consolidation at two
points along the lower tacking edge, disclosed in the condition report annexed to
the registry file.

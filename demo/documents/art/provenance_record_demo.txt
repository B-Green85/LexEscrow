6. CONDITION REPORTS

6.1 REPORT OF MARCH 22, 2007
    Examiner: Auction house conservation department, Thornbury & Vale
    Findings: Canvas taut and unlined. Original stretcher with all keys present.
    Surface with light, even grime consistent with age and display. Minor frame
    abrasion at lower left corner tip, not affecting the picture plane. No
    evidence of retouching under ultraviolet examination. Overall condition
    reported as very good.

6.2 REPORT OF MAY 2, 2019
    Examiner: Redgrave Kestrel International, conservation department
    Findings: Stable overall. Two small points of ground consolidation applied
    along the lower tacking edge, approximately 6 mm and 9 mm in extent, fully
    disclosed and not visible from the recto. Light surface grime. Craquelure
    fine and stable throughout, with no cupping or lifting observed. Varnish
    layer even, moderately discoloured. Condition reported as very good with
    disclosed minor treatment.

6.3 REPORT OF JANUARY 22, 2026
    Examiner: Halloway-Brenner Fine Art Authentication Bureau laboratory
    Findings: Condition unchanged from the 2019 examination. Prior consolidation
    at the lower tacking edge remains sound. No new losses, tears, punctures, or
    deformations. Ultraviolet examination confirms no retouching within the
    picture plane. Recommend surface cleaning and varnish assessment within five
    years. Condition rated very good.

DISPENSARY ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL PARTIES - NOT A LEGAL INSTRUMENT

Agreement No.: APA-2026-GLD-001
Effective Date: September 18, 2026

THIS ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of September
18, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between HORIZON HOLDINGS LLC, a California
limited liability company with offices at 3120 Kestrel Point Way, Suite 400, Demo
City, California 90006 ("Buyer"), and GREENLEAF DISPENSARY LLC, a California
limited liability company holding Cannabis Retailer License No. C10-0001234-LIC,
with its licensed premises at 4500 Commerce Blvd, Demo City, California 90001
("Seller").

RECITALS

A. Seller operates a licensed storefront cannabis retail dispensary at the
licensed premises under License No. C10-0001234-LIC issued by the California
Department of Cannabis Control (the "Department" or "DCC"), and is registered in
the California Cannabis Track-and-Trace system under Metrc Facility ID
MTRC-CA-00042.

B. Buyer desires to acquire the business and assets of Seller, and Seller desires
to sell the same, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

C. The parties acknowledge that consummation of the transactions contemplated
herein requires the prior review and approval of the Department.

1. PURCHASE AND SALE OF ASSETS

1.1 Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, at Closing Seller
shall sell, convey, assign, and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and
acquire from Seller, all of Seller's right, title, and interest in and to the
following (collectively, the "Purchased Assets"):

    (a) all cannabis goods inventory held at the licensed premises and recorded
        in the track-and-trace system under Facility ID MTRC-CA-00042, as
        itemized in the inventory schedule described in Section 3;

    (b) all furniture, fixtures, equipment, display cases, safes, point-of-sale
        hardware, and security systems located at the licensed premises;

    (c) all leasehold improvements at the licensed premises;

    (d) all books, records, standard operating procedures, compliance manuals,
        customer lists, and track-and-trace records relating to the business;

    (e) the trade name "GreenLeaf Dispensary" and all associated goodwill; and

    (f) subject to Department approval, the ownership interests in Seller such
        that License No. C10-0001234-LIC continues in effect under Buyer's
        ownership and control.

1.2 EXCLUDED ASSETS. The Purchased Assets exclude Seller's cash on hand at
Closing, accounts receivable arising prior to Closing, and Seller's organizational
minute books and tax returns, of which copies shall be provided to Buyer.

2. PURCHASE PRICE

2.1 The total purchase price for the Purchased Assets is TWO HUNDRED FIFTY
THOUSAND AND 00/100 DOLLARS.

    Purchase Price: $250,000.00

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid as follows:

    Deposit into Escrow upon Execution ................ $25,000.00
    Balance Payable at Closing ....................... $225,000.00
                                                       ------------
    Total Purchase Price ............................. $250,000.00

2.3 ESCROW. The deposit and the closing funds shall be held by Kestrel Point
Escrow Services, 1900 Halloran Expressway, Suite 210, Demo City, California
90006, Escrow No. KPE-26-3388. The deposit shall be refundable to Buyer in full
if any condition precedent set forth in Section 4 is not satisfied or waived on
or before the Outside Date.

3. ALLOCATION OF PURCHASE PRICE

3.1 The parties agree to allocate the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets
as follows for all tax and reporting purposes:

    ASSET CLASS                                              ALLOCATION
    ----------------------------------------------------   ------------
    Class IV - Cannabis goods inventory (Metrc-tagged)      $250,000.00
    Class V  - Furniture, fixtures, and equipment                 $0.00
    Class V  - Leasehold improvements                             $0.00
    Class VI - Trade name and goodwill                            $0.00
    Class VI - Books, records, and customer lists                 $0.00
    ----------------------------------------------------   ------------
    TOTAL ALLOCATED PURCHASE PRICE                          $250,000.00

3.2 The parties acknowledge that the Purchase Price was determined by reference
to the aggregate documented acquisition cost of Seller's cannabis goods inventory
as reflected in the Metrc inventory schedule, Report No. INV-2026-0912-GLD, dated
September 12, 2026, which states a total inventory purchase price of $250,000.00.

3.3 The furniture, fixtures, equipment, and leasehold improvements described in
Sections 1.1(b) and 1.1(c) are fully depreciated on Seller's books and are
conveyed at no additional consideration. No value is allocated to the license
itself, which is not transferable for value and passes only by Department
approval of the change in ownership.

3.4 Each party shall report the allocation set forth in Section 3.1 consistently
on all tax filings and shall not take any position inconsistent therewith.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT TO CLOSING

The obligation of each party to consummate the transactions contemplated herein
is subject to satisfaction or written waiver of each of the following conditions:

4.1 STATE LICENSE TRANSFER APPROVAL. The Department of Cannabis Control shall
have reviewed and approved the change in ownership of License No.
C10-0001234-LIC, and the license shall remain in ACTIVE status and in good
standing as of the Closing Date. Seller shall submit the required change-of-
ownership notification within five (5) business days of the Effective Date and
both parties shall cooperate diligently in responding to Department inquiries.

4.2 METRC INVENTORY CONFIRMATION. A joint physical inventory count shall be
conducted not more than three (3) business days prior to Closing and reconciled
against the California Cannabis Track-and-Trace system under Facility ID
MTRC-CA-00042. The count must confirm cannabis goods with an aggregate wholesale
value of not less than $250,000.00, all packages bearing PASSED test status, and
a package variance of zero. Any shortfall shall reduce the Purchase Price
dollar-for-dollar.

4.3 REGULATORY COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION. Seller shall deliver written confirmation
that, as of the Closing Date: (a) no administrative hold is placed on any package
or on the license; (b) no enforcement action, citation, accusation, or
disciplinary proceeding is pending or threatened; (c) no fine or penalty is unpaid
and outstanding; (d) all track-and-trace reporting obligations are current; and
(e) all local authorizations, including Local Authorization No. DC-CANN-2024-0088
issued by the City of Demo City, remain in full force and effect.

4.4 LANDLORD CONSENT. Seller shall obtain and deliver the written consent of the
landlord of the licensed premises to the assignment of the premises lease to
Buyer, in form reasonably acceptable to Buyer, together with an estoppel
certificate confirming that no default exists under the lease and stating the
current rent and remaining term.

4.5 OTHER CONDITIONS. (a) The representations and warranties of the other party
shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) no
material adverse change shall have occurred in the business or the licensed
premises; and (c) all deliverables described in Section 6 shall have been
tendered.

5. CLOSING MECHANICS

5.1 CLOSING DATE. The closing of the transactions contemplated herein (the
"Closing") shall occur within ten (10) business days following satisfaction of
the last of the conditions set forth in Section 4, at the offices of the escrow
holder or by electronic exchange of documents.

5.2 OUTSIDE DATE. If Closing has not occurred on or before January 30, 2027 (the
"Outside Date"), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice,
whereupon the escrowed deposit of $25,000.00 shall be returned to Buyer in full
and neither party shall have further obligation, except that the confidentiality
obligations of Section 8.3 survive.

5.3 CLOSING SEQUENCE. At Closing, in the following order: (a) the joint inventory
count under Section 4.2 shall be finalized and signed by both parties; (b) Buyer
shall deposit the balance of $225,000.00 with the escrow holder; (c) Seller shall
deliver the instruments described in Section 6; (d) the escrow holder shall
disburse the Purchase Price to Seller net of prorations and escrow fees; and (e)
the parties shall jointly record the transfer of all cannabis goods packages to
Buyer's control within the track-and-trace system.

5.4 PRORATIONS. Rent, utilities, insurance premiums, and personal property taxes
shall be prorated as of the Closing Date on a 365-day basis.

5.5 ESCROW FEES. Escrow fees shall be divided equally between Buyer and Seller.
Each party shall bear its own legal and accounting fees.

5.6 POSSESSION. Possession of the licensed premises and the Purchased Assets
shall be delivered to Buyer at Closing.

6. CLOSING DELIVERABLES

6.1 Seller shall deliver at Closing: (a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets;
(b) an assignment and assumption of the premises lease with landlord consent; (c)
the final reconciled Metrc inventory schedule signed by Seller's designated
responsible party; (d) the Department's written approval of the change in
ownership; (e) the regulatory compliance certificate described in Section 4.3;
(f) all keys, alarm codes, safe combinations, and track-and-trace system
credentials; and (g) a certificate of Seller's representations and warranties
dated as of the Closing Date.

6.2 Buyer shall deliver at Closing: (a) the balance of the Purchase Price; (b) an
assumption agreement for the premises lease; and (c) a certificate of Buyer's
representations and warranties dated as of the Closing Date.

7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

7.1 Seller represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized and in good
standing under California law; (b) License No. C10-0001234-LIC is valid, active,
and in good standing with an expiration date of January 14, 2027, and Seller has
timely filed its renewal application; (c) Seller owns the Purchased Assets free
and clear of all liens and encumbrances; (d) all cannabis goods included in the
Purchased Assets have passed all required laboratory testing; (e) all
track-and-trace entries under Facility ID MTRC-CA-00042 are accurate and current;
(f) Seller has paid all cannabis excise, sales, and use taxes when due; and (g)
there is no litigation pending or threatened against Seller.

8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
California.

8.2 NO INTEREST PRIOR TO APPROVAL. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed
to transfer to Buyer any ownership, financial interest, or control over the
licensed cannabis business prior to Department approval of the change in
ownership. Seller shall retain sole control of licensed operations until Closing.

8.3 CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this
Agreement and all non-public information of the other party, except as required
by the Department or by law. This Section survives termination for three (3)
years.

8.4 ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the
parties concerning its subject matter and may be amended only in a writing signed
by both parties.

8.5 COUNTERPARTS. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and delivered
electronically, each counterpart being deemed an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective
Date.

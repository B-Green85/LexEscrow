5. PERMITTED ACTIVITIES

5.1 The licensee is authorized to conduct the following commercial cannabis
activities at the licensed premises:

    (a) Retail sale of cannabis and cannabis products to adult-use consumers
        twenty-one (21) years of age or older upon verification of a valid
        government-issued identification;

    (b) Retail sale of medicinal cannabis and cannabis products to qualified
        patients eighteen (18) years of age or older and to primary caregivers,
        upon verification of a valid physician's recommendation or a county-
        issued medical marijuana identification card;

    (c) Purchase of cannabis and cannabis products from licensed distributors
        holding a valid distributor license in good standing;

    (d) Delivery of cannabis goods to customers within the State of California
        by employees of the licensee, using vehicles registered with the
        Department;

    (e) Storage of cannabis goods within the designated limited-access area of
        the licensed premises; and

    (f) Return of cannabis goods to the originating licensed distributor in
        accordance with applicable regulation.

5.2 PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES. The licensee is NOT authorized to cultivate,
manufacture, test, or distribute cannabis, nor to conduct any commercial cannabis
activity at any location other than the licensed premises identified in Section
2.

7. CONDITIONS OF LICENSURE

7.1 TRACK AND TRACE. The licensee shall record all inventory, sales, transfers,
adjustments, and disposals in the California Cannabis Track-and-Trace system
under Facility ID MTRC-CA-00042 within twenty-four (24) hours of the event.

7.2 TESTING. The licensee shall not sell any cannabis goods that have not passed
all required laboratory testing and that are not accompanied by a valid
certificate of analysis.

7.3 PACKAGING AND LABELING. All cannabis goods shall be sold in child-resistant,
tamper-evident packaging bearing all labels required by law, including the
universal symbol and the government warning statement.

7.4 SECURITY. The licensee shall maintain a commercial-grade alarm system, video
surveillance covering all points of entry, all points of sale, and all
limited-access areas, with recordings retained for not less than ninety (90)
days, and a locked safe or vault for after-hours storage of cannabis goods and
currency.

7.5 AGE VERIFICATION. The licensee shall verify the age and identity of every
customer prior to entry into the retail area and again prior to completion of
each sale.

7.6 EMPLOYEE REQUIREMENTS. All employees shall be twenty-one (21) years of age or
older. The licensee shall maintain current employee records and shall comply with
applicable labor peace agreement requirements.

7.7 INVENTORY RECONCILIATION. The licensee shall conduct a full physical
inventory reconciliation not less than once every thirty (30) days and shall
report any discrepancy exceeding the permitted threshold to the Department within
three (3) business days.

7.8 DAILY SALES LIMITS. The licensee shall not sell to any single adult-use
customer in a single day more than 28.5 grams of non-concentrated cannabis or 8
grams of concentrated cannabis.

7.9 RECORDS RETENTION. The licensee shall retain all business records, including
sales invoices, transport manifests, and financial records, for a period of not
less than seven (7) years and shall make them available to the Department upon
request.

7.10 CHANGE OF OWNERSHIP. The licensee shall notify the Department in writing of
any change in ownership structure. Any transfer of ownership interest of twenty
percent (20%) or more requires prior Department review and approval, and the
license itself is not transferable except as expressly approved by the
Department.

7.11 PREMISES MODIFICATION. Any physical modification to the licensed premises
diagram requires prior written approval of the Department.

7.12 INSPECTION. The licensee shall permit Department personnel to enter and
inspect the licensed premises and all records during business hours without prior
notice.

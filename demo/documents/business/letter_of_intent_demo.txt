RE:  PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF VANTAGE SOFTWARE INC.

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "Letter") sets forth the principal terms upon which
NEXBRIDGE CAPITAL PARTNERS, a Delaware limited partnership ("Buyer"), proposes to
acquire VANTAGE SOFTWARE INC., a Delaware corporation ("Seller" or the
"Company"). Except as expressly provided in Sections 6 through 10, this Letter is
NON-BINDING and is intended solely as an expression of mutual interest.

1. STRUCTURE OF TRANSACTION

1.1 Buyer proposes to acquire one hundred percent (100%) of the issued and
outstanding capital stock of the Company from its shareholders in a taxable stock
purchase (the "Transaction").

1.2 The Transaction shall be effected on a cash-free, debt-free basis, with a
normalized net working capital target to be agreed during due diligence and
adjusted dollar-for-dollar at closing.

1.3 The Transaction is intended to be documented in a definitive Stock Purchase
Agreement (the "Definitive Agreement") containing representations, warranties,
covenants, and indemnities customary for a transaction of this size and type.

2. PURCHASE PRICE AND CONSIDERATION

2.1 The aggregate purchase consideration for one hundred percent (100%) of the
equity of the Company shall be:

    Purchase Price: $2,100,000.00

denominated in United States Dollars, payable in immediately available funds at
closing, subject to the adjustments described in Section 2.3.

2.2 EARNEST DEPOSIT. Within five (5) business days of countersignature of this
Letter, Buyer shall deposit with the escrow agent designated under Section 5 the
sum of SIXTY-THREE THOUSAND AND 00/100 DOLLARS ($63,000.00) as an earnest
deposit.

    Earnest Deposit: $63,000.00

The earnest deposit represents three percent (3.0%) of the Purchase Price and
shall be credited against the Purchase Price at closing. The earnest deposit
shall be refundable to Buyer if the Transaction is abandoned for any reason other
than Buyer's unexcused failure to proceed following satisfactory completion of
due diligence.

2.3 ADJUSTMENTS AND HOLDBACK. The Purchase Price is subject to (a) a
dollar-for-dollar working capital adjustment measured against the agreed target;
(b) reduction for indebtedness and transaction expenses outstanding at closing;
and (c) an indemnity holdback of $210,000.00, being ten percent (10%) of the
Purchase Price, to be retained in escrow for a period of twelve (12) months
following closing.

3. DUE DILIGENCE

3.1 Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives full access to the books,
records, contracts, personnel, systems, source code repositories, and facilities
of the Company for a period of THIRTY (30) CALENDAR DAYS commencing on the date
of countersignature of this Letter (the "Due Diligence Period").

3.2 Due diligence shall encompass financial, tax, legal, commercial, technical,
intellectual property, employment, and regulatory review.

3.3 Buyer may terminate the proposed Transaction at any time during the Due
Diligence Period, in Buyer's sole discretion, upon written notice to Seller,
whereupon the earnest deposit shall be returned to Buyer in full.

4. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

4.1 Consummation of the Transaction shall be subject to, among other conditions
to be specified in the Definitive Agreement: (a) satisfactory completion of due
diligence; (b) execution of the Definitive Agreement; (c) receipt of all
requisite third-party and governmental consents; (d) delivery of audited
financial statements for the two most recent fiscal years; (e) execution of
employment and non-competition agreements by the four key employees to be
identified; and (f) absence of any material adverse change.

5. ESCROW

5.1 The earnest deposit and the indemnity holdback shall be held by Sterling
Gateway Fiduciary Services, 1600 Rampart Street, Demo City, Delaware 19803,
Escrow No. SGF-26-2044, pursuant to an escrow agreement to be executed by the
parties.

6. EXCLUSIVITY (BINDING)

6.1 From the date of countersignature of this Letter through the earlier of (a)
the expiration of the Due Diligence Period, or (b) written termination by Buyer,
Seller shall not, and shall cause its officers, directors, shareholders, and
advisors not to, directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, encourage, entertain,
or engage in discussions with any person other than Buyer concerning any sale of
equity or assets, merger, recapitalization, or similar transaction involving the
Company.

6.2 Seller shall notify Buyer within two (2) business days of receipt of any
unsolicited inquiry concerning any such transaction.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY (BINDING)

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential the existence and terms of this Letter and
all non-public information disclosed by the other party, and shall use such
information solely for the purpose of evaluating the Transaction.

7.2 The obligations of this Section survive termination of this Letter for a
period of three (3) years.

8. EXPENSES (BINDING)

8.1 Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses, including fees of counsel,
accountants, and advisors, incurred in connection with the Transaction,
regardless of whether the Transaction is consummated.

9. NO BINDING OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

9.1 Except for Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10, which are intended to be legally
binding, this Letter does not create any legally binding obligation of either
party. No obligation to consummate the Transaction arises unless and until a
Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered by both parties.

10. EXPIRATION AND GOVERNING LAW (BINDING)

10.1 EXPIRATION. This Letter shall expire and be of no force or effect unless a
counterpart signed by Seller is received by Buyer on or before May 18, 2026. If
countersigned, the non-binding provisions of this Letter shall lapse upon the
earlier of execution of the Definitive Agreement or August 15, 2026.

10.2 GOVERNING LAW. This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware
without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

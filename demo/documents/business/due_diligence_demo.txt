1. TRANSACTION OVERVIEW

1.1 This report summarizes the findings of financial, technical, commercial, and
legal due diligence conducted on Vantage Software Inc. (the "Company") in
connection with the proposed acquisition of one hundred percent (100%) of its
outstanding equity.

1.2 Headline consideration under the letter of intent:

    Purchase Consideration (USD): $2,100,000.00
    Earnest Deposit Held in Escrow: $63,000.00
    Indemnity Holdback (12 months): $210,000.00

International B.V., maintains its consolidated statutory reporting in Euros. For
consistency with the parent entity's board reporting package and the shareholder
approval memorandum circulated on May 22, 2026, the purchase consideration is
also stated in Euros as follows:

    Purchase Consideration (EUR): €1,947,600.00

reflecting the reference rate applied by the parent's treasury function as of the
letter of intent date. Closing settlement mechanics, escrow funding, and the
holdback are denominated in United States Dollars under the letter of intent.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSETS

3.1 Registered marks. The Company holds two registered word marks, VANTAGE FLOW
(Reg. No. 7,441,882) and QUILLBRIDGE (Reg. No. 7,502,116), both in International
Class 9, with renewals due in 2032 and 2033 respectively.

3.2 Patents. The Company holds no issued patents. One provisional application
covering an event-stream deduplication method was filed February 11, 2026 and
lapses if not converted by February 11, 2027.

3.3 Copyright and source code. The Company's core platform comprises
approximately 412,000 lines of source code across 14 repositories. A software
composition analysis identified 287 third-party open source dependencies. Of
these, 281 carry permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD). Six carry copyleft
licenses; four are used only in build tooling and are not distributed, and two
are linked into a distributed component and require remediation prior to closing.

3.4 Assignment of inventions. Signed invention assignment agreements were located
for 26 of 31 current employees and for 7 of 11 contractors engaged since 2023.
Missing assignments are itemized in Section 6.

3.5 Domain names and data. The Company holds 9 registered domain names, all
current through at least 2028. Customer data is hosted with a single
infrastructure provider under a contract expiring November 30, 2027.

6. IDENTIFIED RISKS

6.1 HIGH - Customer concentration. Top five accounts at 51.0% of revenue, with
the Tessellate Retail Holdings contract expiring September 30, 2026, prior to the
expiration of the indemnity holdback period. Mitigation: seek early renewal as a
closing condition or negotiate a revenue-based earnout.

6.2 HIGH - Change-of-control consents. Eleven agreements totaling $722,000.00 of
TTM revenue require consent. Mitigation: make receipt of consents covering not
less than $600,000.00 of TTM revenue a condition precedent to closing.

6.3 MEDIUM - Open source license remediation. Two copyleft-licensed components
are linked into distributed software. Estimated remediation cost $28,000.00 and
approximately six engineering weeks. Mitigation: pre-closing remediation covenant
with specific indemnity.

6.4 MEDIUM - Missing invention assignments. Five employees and four contractors
lack executed assignments. Mitigation: obtain executed confirmatory assignments
prior to closing; specific indemnity for any not obtained.

6.5 MEDIUM - Unsupported EBITDA adjustment of $17,000.00. Mitigation: reduce the
agreed adjusted EBITDA figure accordingly for purposes of any multiple-based
price validation.

6.6 MEDIUM - Single-provider infrastructure dependency with contract expiry
November 30, 2027 and no negotiated renewal terms. Mitigation: diligence the
migration cost estimate of $140,000.00 provided by management.

6.7 LOW - Provisional patent application lapses February 11, 2027 if not
converted. Mitigation: budget conversion cost of approximately $16,000.00.

6.8 LOW - Sales tax nexus review identified potential unremitted sales tax
exposure of approximately $34,000.00 across three states. Mitigation: voluntary
disclosure program; specific escrow reserve.

7. VALUATION CONTEXT

7.1 At the letter of intent consideration of $2,100,000.00, implied multiples
are 0.62x TTM revenue and 7.4x adjusted EBITDA. Comparable transactions in the
vertical workflow software segment during the trailing eighteen months cleared
between 0.8x and 1.9x revenue, positioning the proposed consideration below the
observed range, consistent with the concentration and remediation items above.

8. RECOMMENDATION

8.1 Transaction advisory recommends PROCEEDING TO DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT subject to
the mitigations set forth in Section 6, retention of the $210,000.00 indemnity
holdback for the full twelve-month period, and the addition of a specific
indemnity escrow of $50,000.00 for the open source and invention assignment
items.

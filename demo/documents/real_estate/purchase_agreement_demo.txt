THIS RESIDENTIAL PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
as of August 14, 2026, by and between SUNRISE PROPERTIES LLC, a limited liability
company organized under the laws of the State of California, with a mailing
address of 900 Beacon Hollow Road, Suite 310, Demo City, California 90003
("Seller"), and THOMAS WHITFIELD and CLAIRE WHITFIELD, husband and wife, residing
at 1815 Marigold Terrace, Demo City, California 90004 (collectively, "Buyer").

1. PROPERTY

1.1 Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to purchase, upon the terms and
conditions set forth herein, that certain improved real property commonly known
as:

    4271 Oakridge Lane, Clearwater County, California 90007
    Assessor's Parcel Number (APN): 8842-317-024

together with all improvements, fixtures, and appurtenances thereto (the
"Property").

1.2 The Property consists of a single-family residence of approximately 2,640
square feet situated on a lot of approximately 0.41 acres, together with an
attached two-car garage.

2. PURCHASE PRICE AND PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 The total purchase price for the Property shall be:

    Purchase Price: $485,000.00

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be payable as follows:

    Earnest Money Deposit ............................. $14,550.00
    Additional Cash Down at Closing ................... $82,450.00
    New First Mortgage Loan Proceeds .................. $388,000.00
                                                       ------------
    Total Consideration ............................... $485,000.00

2.3 EARNEST MONEY. Within three (3) business days following mutual execution of
this Agreement, Buyer shall deposit the sum of FOURTEEN THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED
FIFTY AND 00/100 DOLLARS ($14,550.00) with the Escrow Holder identified in
Section 5. The earnest money deposit represents three percent (3.0%) of the
Purchase Price and shall be credited to Buyer at closing.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The closing of the transaction contemplated herein (the "Closing") shall
occur on or before October 20, 2026 (the "Closing Date"), at the offices of the
Escrow Holder or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 Possession of the Property shall be delivered to Buyer at 5:00 p.m. local
time on the Closing Date, free of all tenancies and occupants.

3.3 Time is of the essence with respect to the Closing Date. The Closing Date may
be extended only by written agreement signed by both parties.

4. CONTINGENCIES

4.1 FINANCING CONTINGENCY. Buyer's obligation to close is contingent upon Buyer
obtaining a first mortgage loan in the principal amount of $388,000.00 at an
interest rate not exceeding seven and one-quarter percent (7.25%) per annum, on a
thirty (30) year amortization. Buyer shall have forty-five (45) calendar days
from the date of this Agreement (the "Financing Contingency Period") to obtain a
written loan commitment. If Buyer, acting in good faith and with diligence, is
unable to obtain such commitment within the Financing Contingency Period, Buyer
may terminate this Agreement by written notice and the earnest money deposit
shall be returned to Buyer in full.

4.2 INSPECTION CONTINGENCY. Buyer shall have seventeen (17) calendar days from
the date of this Agreement (the "Inspection Period") to conduct, at Buyer's sole
expense, such inspections of the Property as Buyer deems appropriate, including
general home inspection, roof, structural, pest, sewer lateral, and environmental
inspections. If the inspections disclose conditions unacceptable to Buyer, Buyer
may, prior to expiration of the Inspection Period: (a) terminate this Agreement
by written notice, whereupon the earnest money deposit shall be returned to
Buyer; or (b) deliver a written request for repairs or credit, which Seller may
accept or decline in Seller's discretion.

4.3 APPRAISAL CONTINGENCY. Buyer's obligation to close is further contingent upon
the Property appraising at a value not less than the Purchase Price of
$485,000.00 by an appraiser acceptable to Buyer's lender.

4.4 Failure of Buyer to deliver written notice of termination prior to expiration
of any contingency period shall be deemed a waiver of that contingency.

5. TITLE AND ESCROW

5.1 ESCROW HOLDER. The parties designate Clearwater County Title & Escrow
Services, 415 Meridian Court, Demo City, California 90005, Escrow No.
CCT-26-88417, as the escrow holder ("Escrow Holder"). This Agreement, together
with any supplemental instructions, shall constitute joint escrow instructions.

5.2 TITLE. Seller shall convey to Buyer marketable fee simple title by grant
deed, free and clear of all liens, encumbrances, easements, and restrictions
other than: (a) current-year real property taxes not yet delinquent; (b)
covenants, conditions, and restrictions of record that do not materially impair
use of the Property as a single-family residence; and (c) utility easements of
record.

5.3 TITLE INSURANCE. Seller shall furnish at Seller's expense a CLTA standard
coverage owner's policy of title insurance in the amount of $485,000.00 issued by
a title insurer licensed in the State of California. Any additional premium for
extended or ALTA coverage requested by Buyer or Buyer's lender shall be borne by
Buyer.

5.4 ESCROW FEES. Escrow fees shall be divided equally between Seller and Buyer.
Recording fees shall be paid by Buyer. County and city transfer taxes shall be
paid by Seller.

6. PRORATIONS

6.1 Real property taxes, assessments, homeowners association dues, and utility
charges shall be prorated as of the Closing Date on the basis of a 365-day year.

7. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

7.1 If Buyer defaults, Seller's sole and exclusive remedy shall be to retain the
earnest money deposit of $14,550.00 as liquidated damages, the parties agreeing
that actual damages would be impracticable to determine.

7.2 If Seller defaults, Buyer may elect to (a) terminate this Agreement and
recover the earnest money deposit in full, or (b) pursue specific performance.

8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
California.

8.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement contains the entire understanding of the
parties and supersedes all prior negotiations. No amendment is effective unless
in a writing signed by both parties.

8.3 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which
is deemed an original, and delivered electronically.
